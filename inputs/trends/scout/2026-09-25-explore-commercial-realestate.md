# Scout snapshot 2026-09-25

Source: Instagram Explore and the #commercialrealestate hashtag page, signed in, read-only. Small sample: about 4 real-estate posts out of roughly 22 tiles. Explore is personalised to the Shrih Plaza account, so this is inspiration, not a national ranking.

## Usable patterns
- Education posts that name a common misconception first, then give a short step chain (for example a leads-to-visits-to-deal funnel), work as text-led carousels.
- Local-news style hooks open with an emoji alert and one plain sentence, then list the places affected.
- Competing SCO projects lead with three practical selling points in one line: front-facing showrooms, location, parking. Shrih Plaza can do this with its own verified points (SH-11, central parking, signed brands).
- Short prompts that invite a comment ("comment a word to get...") are common on design and marketing accounts and lift reach.

## Content angles
- Why front-facing frontage and parking matter for a retail SCO.
- What signed brands mean for footfall (only the 12 approved brands, no numbers).
- Construction nearing completion plus limited SCO spaces, as an honest update.
- Festival greetings that still name the location.

## Formats
- carousel (education, step chain)
- reel (site walk-through)
- static (single strong headline)

## Avoid copying
- Return language such as "high return potential". It is a forbidden claim for Shrih Plaza.
- Bare phone-number lead posts with "multiple options, multiple locations". Low premium feel.
- Any competitor's exact wording, location names or artwork.

## Observations
Nearby competitor content is mostly plain and broker-style. A clean, branded, single-message post like the Limited SCO hero already stands out from it.
