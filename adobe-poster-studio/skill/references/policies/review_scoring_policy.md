# Review Scoring Policy

Every generated content item receives a score from 0 to 10.

## Required Minimum Scores

- Fact accuracy: 10
- Architecture safety: 10
- Legal/RERA safety: 10
- Brand match: 8
- Visual quality: 8
- Trend fit: 7
- Sales clarity: 7

If any required score fails, the item must be sent to the Auto-Fix Agent.

## Auto-Reject Conditions

- Any unverified factual claim
- Any forbidden real estate claim
- Architecture changed
- Project name wrong
- Logo misuse
- Spelling or grammar errors
- Design feels generic or low quality
- Caption copies a competitor too closely
- CTA missing

## Human Review Conditions

Ask the user instead of auto-fixing when:

- A new fact is needed
- Legal wording is unclear
- The image asset itself is low quality
- The user must choose between two creative directions
- The system repeatedly fails after three fix attempts
