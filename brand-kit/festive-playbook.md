# Festive × Shrih Plaza playbook (owner direction, 2026-10-03)

Festive posts put **Shrih Plaza itself inside the festival**, the way the owner's own Ganesh, Janmashtami and
Independence Day posts did. They wish people first and sell second: the greeting leads, the ownership line is soft,
and the enquiry stays small.

## What works (references from other real-estate brands on Instagram, Oct 2026)

- **Fuse the festival symbol with the property.** Magicbricks lit a tower's windows so they form a diya; Niwas drew a
  house outline in light around Durga. For us: the festival's symbol acts on the plaza (an arrow of victory landing
  on the SHRIH PLAZA sign, diyas along the forecourt edge, a rangoli in the fountain courtyard, lanterns over the rooftops).
- **Map the festival icon to real features.** Durga's hands each holding a benefit. Use only approved facts (retail
  shops, offices, central parking, SH-11 address, RERA approved, limited SCO spaces). Never "smart investment" or returns.
- **Minimal iconic motifs** (Durga's eyes, a single diya, a bow) on brand colours, rather than clip-art collages.
- **Blessings walking in.** Footprints or light entering a shop doorway.
- **Bilingual greeting.** Hindi or Punjabi line in a proper Devanagari or Gurmukhi font + English headline.
- **Series formats.** Navratri day 1-9 posts fit our one-piece-a-day cadence; a teaser before a big festival also works.

## Rules

1. The building stays the hero and untouched. Festive elements live in the sky, the frame or the foreground.
2. Greeting first. Ownership line soft ("This festive season, begin your own story on SH-11."), then the enquiry.
3. Deities only with respect: full figures, never cropped oddly, never used as decoration for a sales line, never
   distorted. Prefer symbols (diya, bow and arrow, marigold toran, kalash, rangoli) when unsure.
4. No festival offers, discounts or "auspicious time to invest" claims unless the owner approves them. The legal
   agent's banned phrases still apply.
5. All standing rules apply: gold logo, sans-serif phone, full-height numerals (no old-style "11"), nothing
   overlapping graphics, "opening soon" never "coming", all 12 logos if the 12 brands are mentioned.
6. Every festival gets a new idea. Never repeat last year's or another festival's layout.
