### Palette freedom (owner decision, 2026-09-25)

Navy and gold is the default, not a limit. Posts and the Studio may use any colour. Presets built in: navy (default), emerald (the original website green), maroon (festive), charcoal and plum. Rules that still hold:

- Keep a dark ground behind the gold logo so it stays readable. A light post needs a dark header band for the logo.
- Text needs at least 4.5:1 contrast.
- Gold stays the accent so the brand still reads as Shrih Plaza.
- Match the occasion: maroon or plum for festivals, emerald for a heritage look, navy for investment and leasing.

### Construction and availability wording

Use these lines as the urgency hook. They are real, so they are not "fake urgency":

```text
Construction nearing completion. Limited SCO spaces available.
```

```text
Limited SCO spaces available. Book a site visit.
```

Rules:

- No completion date, month or percentage.
- No unit count and no "only N left".
- Never "possession soon", "hurry" or "sold out soon". Possession dates need separate proof.
- Renders on posts still carry "Artist's impression" while construction is not finished.
- Remove or update both lines the day construction completes or the SCOs are fully allotted. A stale availability claim is a compliance risk.

## Premium Poster Standard (owner approved 2026-09-26)

The owner approved the premium redesign of poster #1 and asked to keep raising the level.

**Rule (owner, 2026-09-26): an approved design is a quality bar, never a template.** Every new post must be a completely new design: a new layout family, a new photo or crop and a new type treatment. Putting new text on an approved design is not a new post. `memory/design_history.json` records every approved design, and the visual reviewer fails any post that repeats one.

The quality bar every new design must meet or beat:

- Photo: a golden-hour or dusk render. Shop signage inside the renders is fine (owner rule 2026-09-26); choose photos for design quality only.
- Frame: thin gold inset frame with corner marks.
- Seal: circular gold RERA APPROVED / FULLY APPROVED seal, top right. Logo top left.
- Type: one hero word in Playfair Display Bold Italic (gold), with Poppins Light in wide tracking for the line under it and Poppins SemiBold for labels and the phone. One italic Playfair supporting line.
- Boxes: fact panels with a thin gold top edge on a translucent dark ground, a title and one short line each.
- Call to action: split block, gold "BOOK A SITE VISIT" beside a dark panel with a gold border for the phone.
- Always: "Artist's impression." while construction is not finished.
