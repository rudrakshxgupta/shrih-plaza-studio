# Shrih Plaza content strategy (owner brief, 2026-10-01)

The job is not just attractive social content. Every post uses brands, project progress, location
and commercial activity to create buyer interest in **owning a shop at Shrih Plaza**.

> Don't just show people why they should visit Shrih Plaza. Show them why they should want to own a shop there.

## The flow every post follows

**BRAND → LOCATION → COMMERCIAL ACTIVITY → SHOP OWNERSHIP → ENQUIRY**

The brand gets attention. The project gets consideration. The shop gets the enquiry.

1. **Hook**: the recognisable brand or visual stops the scroll. "Recognise the name? Now imagine owning the space around it."
2. **Brand / social proof**: show the brand prominently, but it is never the whole message.
3. **Commercial logic**: connect the brand to the development. "Leading brands choose locations where visibility and accessibility matter." "Where brands come, businesses follow."
4. **Ownership transition** (the most important step): move from "I like this brand" to "I could own a shop here". "Don't just visit the destination. Own a part of it." "Customers may come for the brand. You could own the space."
5. **CTA**: clear and premium. "Own your commercial space at Shrih Plaza." "Enquire for shop availability." "DM us to explore available shops."

A brand post never ends on "Visit Domino's", "Enjoy your coffee" or "Shop at Jockey". It always returns to
Shrih Plaza + commercial space + ownership + enquiry.

## Carousels and reels

- Frame 1: big brand / visual hook ("DOMINO'S")
- Frame 2: Shrih Plaza, an emerging commercial destination in Dhuri
- Frame 3: ownership: "Customers come for the brands. Owners own the opportunity."
- Frame 4: CTA: "Own your commercial space at Shrih Plaza."

## Taglines

For every brand, image, update or announcement, offer 5–10 tagline options across these angles:
ownership, brand association, location, aspiration, investment/ownership, commercial activity, and
scarcity (only when real availability supports it; "Limited SCO spaces are available" is approved).

## Tone

Premium, intelligent, confident, aspirational, commercially sophisticated. Never desperate, cheap,
spammy, overly salesy or unrealistic. Never: "BUY NOW!!!", "BEST INVESTMENT", "100% RETURNS",
"GUARANTEED PROFIT", "DOUBLE YOUR MONEY", or any return/appreciation promise.

## For every input, decide

1. The strongest attention hook. 2. The commercial psychology. 3. How the hook connects to Shrih Plaza.
4. How the viewer moves toward shop ownership. 5. A curiosity tagline. 6. The CTA.
7. What appears on the visual. 8. What the caption says.

## Facts stay accurate

Only the 12 approved brands may be featured (Domino's, The Brew Estate, Basant Ice Cream & Dessert Bar,
VW Mart 24x7 Cafe & Grocery Store, Octave, Barista, Sagar Ratna, Tress Lounge Salon, Jockey, Elini,
Purple Kids, Baba's Chicken). The owner's brief also names Monte Carlo, which is not on the approved
list: do not feature it until the owner confirms it is signed.

## Content mix (owner rule, 2026-10-01)

Every content set has three pieces:

1. **Shrih Plaza only**: the project, location and ownership, no brands.
2. **One big brand x Shrih Plaza**: one of the approved 12, brand first, ending on owning a shop. Rotate the featured brand each set.
3. **All 12 brands x Shrih Plaza**: every signed brand's logo with the Shrih Plaza logo, ending on ownership and enquiry.

## Posting cadence (owner plan, 2026-10-02)

One piece per day: a feed post **or** a reel. The profile grid builds pictures in blocks of
**3, 6, 3, 9, 3** days (a 24-day cycle): a one-row panorama strip, a 3×2 picture, a strip, a 3×3
picture, a strip. Every block is a new picture with a new theme. Within a block, the bottom-right tile is posted first
and the top-left last. Posts are carousels (slide 1 = grid piece, slide 2 = the design); reels use
the grid piece as their cover. Mix posts and reels about half and half, and rotate the content
mix day by day.
